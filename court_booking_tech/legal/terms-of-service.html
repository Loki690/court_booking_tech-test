1. What BookPickleBall is

BookPickleBall is a booking platform operated by BYTEUNITY SOLUTIONS & SERVICES CORP. We let you find sports facilities and reserve court time at them.

We are not the facility. Each facility is an independent business that sets its own prices, opening hours, house rules, and cancellation and refund policy. We provide the software that takes the booking. The court time itself is provided by the facility, and your booking is an agreement between you and that facility.

2. Your account

  • You need an account to book. Create one with an email and password, or sign in with Google.
  • Keep your login details to yourself. You are responsible for activity under your account.
  • One person, one account. Do not create accounts to evade a suspension.
  • Give accurate contact details — a facility may need to reach you about your booking.

3. Bookings

  • A booking is confirmed when the facility accepts it, which normally means your payment has been verified. Until then a slot is held for a limited time and is released automatically if payment is not completed.
  • Prices, opening hours and available courts are set by the facility and shown to you before you confirm.
  • Arrive on time. A facility may treat a no-show according to its own rules.

4. Payment

  • Payment methods vary by facility and may include bank or e-wallet transfer with proof of payment, or cash at the front desk.
  • We do not process card payments and do not store card or bank account numbers.
  • Where you pay by transfer, you upload proof and the facility verifies it. A booking that is not verified in time may be released.
  • Some facilities charge a booking fee in addition to court time. Where charged, it is shown as a separate line before you confirm.

5. Changes, cancellations and refunds

Each facility sets its own cancellation and refund policy, and that policy governs your booking. Some facilities refund a paid booking. Others move it to another time, or give you credit to spend at that facility instead. Where a facility does not refund cash, that is shown to you at checkout and on your booking before you pay.

If a facility cancels because of a problem on its own side — a closed court, a burst pipe — it may return your payment even where its usual policy is to reschedule.

Nothing on this page is a promise of a refund by us. Where a facility agrees to a refund or issues account credit, we provide the software to record it; the commercial decision is the facility's.

Rescheduling is handled by the facility's staff — contact the facility.
